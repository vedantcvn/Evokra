About Evokra

We build the connective tissue between a team's intent and the software that executes it.

01 — Operators first

Every engagement starts with the work itself, not the model. We shadow your process, then build only what removes friction.

02 — You own the system

Code, credentials, prompts, workflows — yours on day one. No platform lock-in, no opaque pipelines.

Capabilities

realities of real work.

Multi-agent orchestration

Specialist agents that delegate, debate, and converge on answers — coordinated by a supervisor agent you can prompt-edit.

RAG with citations

Human-in-the-loop
